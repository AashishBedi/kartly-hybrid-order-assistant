# Warranty Policy

## Warranty period

Electronics include the warranty period shown on the product record. Kartly electronics normally carry either a 12-month or 24-month warranty. The warranty begins on the recorded delivery date, not the order date or shipment date. The product's stated `warranty_months` value determines the exact period for that item.

Non-electronic products have no warranty unless a warranty is specifically stated for that product. A return window and a warranty are separate protections. Expiry of the 30-day return window does not shorten an active electronics warranty.

## Covered problems

The warranty covers manufacturing defects that cause the product to fail during normal use. Examples include an internal component failure, a device that no longer powers on because of a manufacturing fault, or a control that stops operating under normal conditions.

The warranty does not cover physical damage, cracked screens, dents, misuse, accidental drops, unauthorised repairs, normal wear, or water and other liquid damage. Cosmetic changes that do not affect operation are not manufacturing defects.

## Making a claim

A warranty claim should include the order number, product name, description of the problem, and any requested photos or diagnostic details. Kartly checks the delivery date and the product's warranty period before reviewing the reported defect.

An approved claim may result in repair, replacement, or another remedy appropriate to the defect. A final-sale electronics item keeps its stated warranty even though it cannot be returned or exchanged under the standard return rules.
