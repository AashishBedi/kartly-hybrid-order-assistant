# Exchange Policy

## Exchange window

Eligible items may be exchanged within the same 30-calendar-day window used for returns. The window is counted from the recorded delivery date. The customer must request the exchange before the window ends and before sending the item back.

The item must be unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging with labels and included parts. Items marked final sale cannot be exchanged. An order that has not been delivered is not eligible for exchange.

## Size and colour exchanges

An exchange is available only for a different size or colour of the same product. The requested option must be in stock when the exchange is processed. Stock is not reserved merely because an exchange request has been submitted.

If the requested size or colour is unavailable, the customer may choose another available option of the same product or proceed with an eligible return. Kartly sends the replacement after the returned item is received and its condition is checked.

## Different products

Changing to a different product is not treated as an exchange. The original item must be handled as a return under the Returns Policy, and the desired product must be purchased as a new order. Any refund for the original item is issued only after receipt and inspection.

Damaged or wrong items follow the separate Damaged or Wrong Items policy. When Kartly confirms that problem, replacement delivery and return shipping are provided at no cost rather than handled as a preference-based size or colour exchange.
