# Damaged or Wrong Items

## Reporting the problem

An item that arrives damaged or is different from the item ordered must be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery. The delivery date recorded on the order starts this period. Reports made after 7 days may not qualify for the damaged-or-wrong-item process, though an electronics manufacturing defect may still be reviewed under an active warranty.

The report must include the order number, a description of the problem, and at least one clear photo showing the damaged item or the incorrect item received. Keep the product, packaging, labels, and included accessories until Kartly provides instructions. Photos help confirm the issue and identify damage that may have occurred before delivery.

## Available resolution

After reviewing the report, Kartly will offer a replacement or a refund at no cost when the item is confirmed as damaged on arrival or incorrect. A replacement is subject to stock availability. If a replacement is unavailable, Kartly will issue a refund to the original payment method after the returned item is received and inspected.

## Return shipping

Return shipping is free for a confirmed damaged or wrong item. Kartly provides the return instructions or shipping label. The customer should not pay for an unapproved shipping method or send the item before receiving those instructions.

Original shipping charges are refundable for confirmed damaged or wrong items. This process applies even when the affected product is normally marked final sale, because receiving a damaged or incorrect item is not a standard preference-based return.
