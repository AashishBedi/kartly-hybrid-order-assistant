# Final-Sale Policy

## Identifying final-sale items

Clearance products may be marked as final sale. The final-sale status is displayed on the product page before purchase and is stored with the product as the `is_final_sale` flag. Customers should review this label before placing an order because the restriction is based on the product's status at the time of purchase.

## Return and exchange restrictions

An item marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged. The normal 30-day return window does not override this restriction. Final-sale clothing cannot be exchanged for another size or colour, and a final-sale item cannot be returned in order to purchase a different product.

The restriction applies even if the item is unused and remains in its original packaging. A request based only on preference, fit, colour, or change of mind is not eligible for approval.

## Problems with the item

Final sale does not remove protections for an item that arrives damaged or is not the item ordered. Those problems must be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery with a photo and are handled under the Damaged or Wrong Items policy.

Final-sale electronics also keep the warranty stated on the product. A 12-month or 24-month electronics warranty continues from the delivery date and covers qualifying manufacturing defects. It does not cover physical damage, water damage, or misuse. Warranty service is separate from a return or exchange, so keeping the warranty does not make the final-sale product returnable.
