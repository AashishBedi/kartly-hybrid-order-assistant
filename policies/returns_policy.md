# Returns Policy

## Return window

Most eligible items may be returned within 30 calendar days of the delivery date shown on the order. The 30-day period begins on the day after delivery. An order that has not been delivered is not returnable. If its status is `placed`, use the cancellation process instead. An order with the status `shipped` must first be delivered before a return can be considered.

## Item condition

The item must be unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging with all included parts, labels, manuals, and accessories. Clothing may be tried on for fit, but it must not show wear, washing, stains, or removed tags. Kartly may reject a return when the item is incomplete or its condition prevents resale.

Products marked with `is_final_sale` are final-sale items and cannot be returned. This restriction applies even when the return request is made within 30 days.

## Starting a return

A return request must be submitted and approved before the item is sent back. Include the order number, the item being returned, and the reason for the request. Do not send an item without return instructions, because it may not be identified or accepted.

After approval, pack the item securely and follow the provided shipping instructions. Receiving a return request within the window does not guarantee acceptance. The returned item is checked against the eligibility and condition rules before a refund or exchange is approved.
