# Refund Policy

## Refund timing

Kartly issues an approved refund only after the returned item has been received and inspected. The inspection confirms that the returned product matches the order and meets the applicable return conditions. Customers are notified after the inspection is complete and the refund decision has been made.

An approved refund is sent to the original payment method. It normally appears within 5–7 business days after Kartly receives the returned item. A bank or payment provider may take additional time to display the credit after Kartly has issued it. Kartly does not redirect a refund to a different card, bank account, or payment method.

## Refund amount

The refundable product amount is based on the unit price paid for the accepted item and the quantity returned. Original shipping charges are refunded only when Kartly confirms that the item was defective or that the wrong item was sent. Shipping charges are not refunded for preference-based returns, such as a change of mind, size selection, or colour selection.

## Inspection results

If an item fails inspection because it is used, damaged after delivery, incomplete, or missing its original packaging, Kartly may deny the refund. Final-sale items are also ineligible for a standard return refund.

When only part of an order is returned, the refund covers only the accepted returned items. A returned order is not considered refunded merely because its shipment has reached Kartly; the refund is issued after inspection and approval.
