# Payment and Invoices

## Accepted payment methods

Kartly accepts credit and debit cards, UPI, and net banking. Payment must be authorised before an order can proceed normally. The payment provider may ask the customer to complete an authentication step, such as a one-time password or approval in a banking application.

Kartly never stores full card numbers. Card details are handled by the payment provider, and Kartly retains only limited transaction information needed to identify the payment and support the order.

## Failed payments

When a payment attempt fails, Kartly automatically retries it once. The retry uses the same payment instruction and does not create a second order. If the automatic retry also fails, the payment remains unsuccessful and the customer must use an accepted payment method to place a successful order.

A pending bank entry is not proof that Kartly received the payment. Banks may temporarily show an authorisation after a failed attempt. The bank normally releases an unsuccessful authorisation according to its own processing time.

## Invoices

An invoice is emailed when the order status becomes `shipped`. The invoice includes the order number, purchased items, quantities, unit prices, applicable charges, and total paid. Customers should retain the invoice for returns, damaged-item reports, and electronics warranty claims.

If an order contains several items shipped together, they appear on the same invoice. A refund does not replace the original invoice; refund confirmation separately records the amount returned to the original payment method.
