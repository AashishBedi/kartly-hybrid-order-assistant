# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

Orders normally ship within 1 business day of being placed. Processing time is separate from delivery time. Orders remain in the `placed` status while they are being prepared. When the package leaves the fulfilment facility, the status changes to `shipped` and tracking information is sent to the customer.

Tracking may take several hours to show its first carrier scan. The `shipped` status means the package has entered the delivery network; it does not mean the order has been delivered.

## Delivery estimates

Standard shipping normally takes 5–7 business days after shipment. Express shipping normally takes 1–2 business days after shipment. These estimates use business days and do not count weekends or public holidays.

Addresses in remote areas require 2 additional business days. Therefore, a remote-area standard delivery normally takes 7–9 business days after shipment, while a remote-area express delivery normally takes 3–4 business days after shipment.

## Delivery status

An order changes to `delivered` when the carrier confirms delivery. The delivery date recorded on the order is used for time-sensitive policies, including return and damage-reporting windows.

Carrier delays, severe weather, or access problems may cause delivery to take longer than the estimate. Customers should use the tracking details sent at shipment to follow the package. If tracking shows delivery but the package cannot be located, the customer should report the issue with the order number and tracking details.
